The Dr. Victor A. Bacile Scholarship Program supports college students connected to our community.

The Bacile legacy

Helping students
take their next step.

The center awards up to four scholarships of $4,000 each annually. The program honors Dr. Victor A. Bacile, a former center president and local physician.

Scholarships support eligible college students who are members or qualifying family members.
