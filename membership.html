Before you apply

Who can become a member?

Regular membership

For people of Italian birth or descent who are U.S. citizens or aspire to become citizens.

Non-Italian membership

For U.S. citizens who are spouses of people of Italian birth or descent.

Applicants must be at least 18, be of good character, and have a recommendation from a regular member in good standing.

Your next chapter starts here

How to join

  1. Submit your application

    Download the form and follow its instructions.

  2. Meet the committee

    An informal interview covers your background and interest in joining.

  3. Welcome to the center

    Committee approval is followed by a membership vote and induction.
